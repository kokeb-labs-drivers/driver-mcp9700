 The error limits the datasheet guarantees at a temperature (first band that contains it, narrowest first). With High only the High-option rows (-40 to +150 C) apply over the whole range: the datasheet does not say that an H part also meets the tighter Extended rows. For a named variant they hold at its tested supply; the driver never applies them to a reading.
